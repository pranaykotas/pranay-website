Public Policy

Indian state capacity, regulation, federalism, political economy, and policy frameworks

Public policy is the throughline of all my work. I research India’s state capacity puzzles, regulatory quality, competitive federalism, and the political economy of reform. My goal is to make policy frameworks accessible to students, practitioners, and curious readers.

Book

Missing in Action: Why You Should Care About Public Policy (Penguin India, 2023) is a primer on Indian public policy through stories, frameworks, and Bollywood references.

Frameworks

A plain-language collection of public policy frameworks and mental models for thinking about policy problems.
