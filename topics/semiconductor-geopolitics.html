Semiconductor Geopolitics

Chips, fabs, supply chains, and the geopolitics of semiconductor manufacturing

Semiconductors sit at the intersection of technology, economics, national security, trade, and industrial policy. This page brings together my work on chip supply chains, India’s semiconductor strategy, and the geopolitics of technological interdependence.
