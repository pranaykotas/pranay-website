India Foreign Policy

India’s neighbourhood policy, the Quad, great power dynamics, and strategic autonomy

India’s foreign policy operates at the intersection of strategic autonomy, neighbourhood management, and great power alignment. I write on India’s relations with China, Pakistan, and the countries of the Indian subcontinent, the Quad and Indo-Pacific strategy, and how domestic political economy shapes foreign policy choices.

Book
